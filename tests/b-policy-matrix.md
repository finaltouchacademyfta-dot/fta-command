### Row filters and redaction (teachers and students)

- **Teacher (Mentor):** receives only records tagged with their own teacher id. Tags are computed **on the server** from the record (`MEN-…`/`STU-…` ids, class-group membership, enrolment teacher), never trusted from the browser, and refreshed when a class group or enrolment changes. Students: no office notes; enrolments: no fees, discounts; programmes: no fee; batches: no teacher pay; other teachers: name, subjects and status only; payroll: only their own lines (who, method, amount); no invoices, payments, expenses, accounts, transfers, budgets, bank balances or academy share. May write attendance, homework, class notes, marks (not approve), leave reports, events, routine of their own classes; may only *request* a student portal (saved inactive until the office approves).
- **Student:** only their own student record, enrolments (no fees), class groups (showing only themself), attendance and remarks for themself, approved/published marks (own score only), homework for them, class notes for them or the whole class, published notices, online exams for their class (answer keys stripped), their own attempts (start time and deadline set by the server, marks kept by the server). Can change nothing else.
- **Collection Staff:** students (read), invoices and payments; may create and edit **Pending** payments only (Finance verifies); follow-ups and notes. Teachers: name, subjects, phone; accounts: name and type only.
- **Finance Manager:** all money collections and people records; cannot change school records, permissions, settings or sign-in accounts.
- **Junior Admin:** front office (people, school, notices); no money except creating Pending payments; cannot change fees or discounts; cannot delete; can give sign-in access to teachers and students only.
- **Admin:** everything except Owner-only settings (permissions, organisation, papers, messages, look) and cannot add, change or remove the Owner or other Admins.
- **Owner:** everything. Only the Owner can add or remove Admins. The Owner cannot remove themself, and the last active Owner can never be removed (database trigger).
- **Viewer:** reads office data (not the audit trail), changes nothing.
- **Everyone:** a change is refused if the record changed on the server since the person last saw it (optimistic concurrency → “someone else changed this”); activity log and audit lines are append-only.
